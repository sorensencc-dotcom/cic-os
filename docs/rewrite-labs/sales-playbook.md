# Rewrite Labs Sales Playbook
**Scripts • Frameworks • Objection Handling • Closing**

---

## Core Sales Narrative

Rewrite Labs is:
- Faster than agencies
- Cheaper than freelancers
- Higher quality than templates
- More scalable than in-house teams

Your job: help prospects see that.

---

## 3-Step Sales Framework

### 1. Diagnose
"What's the biggest frustration with your current website?"

### 2. Demonstrate
"We generated a redesign for you automatically — here's what it looks like."

### 3. Deliver
"We can deploy this for you in days, not months."

---

## The Pitch (Short)

"We redesign your website automatically using AI.
You get a modern, mobile-friendly, conversion-optimized site — without the cost or delay of a traditional agency."

## Objection Handling

### "I already have a website."
"That's exactly why we reached out — your site works, but it's not modern. We can upgrade it without disrupting your business."

### "I don't have time."
"That's the beauty of Rewrite Labs — we do everything for you."

### "Is this custom?"
"Yes. Every redesign is generated specifically for your industry, your content, and your brand."

### "How much does it cost?"
"We're a fraction of traditional agencies. Starts at $49/month for startups, scales with your needs."
