# Rewrite Labs Partner Program Guide

For agencies, resellers, and integration partners.

---

## **1. Program Overview**
Rewrite Labs Partner Program enables agencies, studios, and resellers to deliver AI‑powered redesigns at scale.

## **2. Partner Tiers**

### **Tier 1 — Registered Partner**
- Access to dashboard  
- Basic support  
- 10% revenue share  

### **Tier 2 — Certified Partner**
- White‑label platform  
- Priority support  
- 20% revenue share  
- Training & certification  

### **Tier 3 — Elite Partner**
- Dedicated account manager  
- Co‑marketing  
- 30% revenue share  
- Early access to new features
